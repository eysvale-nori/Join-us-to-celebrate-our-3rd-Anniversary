For You, My Mariel

My Mariel,

Three years ago, I never could have imagined how many beautiful memories we would create together. We've laughed, we've grown, we've had difficult days, and we've experienced moments that I will remember for the rest of my life.

The reason why I choose you again is because, deep inside my heart, I already know that you are the one. And after three years, I can say that my heart was right. Why? Because you have always been here for me, no matter what. Through every up and down, we held each other's hands and never let go. We help one another grow, reach our dreams, and become successful. We started building our own future together, little by little. So let's keep holding on to each other, because I know that one day, we will make it. We will achieve everything we dreamed of together. Thank you for your unconditional love. Thank you for never failing to send me and fetch me from work every day. Thank you for always being with me wherever I go. Thank you for cooking real and delicious food for me, and for taking care of me whenever I am sick or not feeling well. Thank you for always making me smile. Thank you for understanding me in every aspect of my life. Most especially, thank you for respecting my decisions. You never interfere with the dreams and choices I make because you understand that many of them are dreams I have carried since I was a child. You support me, you trust me, and you allow me to become the person I always wanted to be.

You never fail to amaze me with everything you do, and I truly appreciate you more than words can explain. I still remember when we were just starting. You were grumpy. You had a short temper. You couldn't easily express your true feelings, and whenever something was bothering you, you would hide it instead of telling me what was wrong. But throughout these three years, I watched you grow. I tried to help you become a better person. I taught you some of the realities of life, helped you understand your emotions, and encouraged you to open your heart and share your thoughts without hesitation. And now, I am so happy because you finally know how to express yourself. You know how to control your emotions. You know how to communicate your thoughts and feelings. You learned how to open up to me. But I also want you to know that I didn't build you alone. You also chose to build yourself.You helped yourself become better, and you allowed me to be part of that journey. And that is something I will always be proud of.

I don't know exactly what the future will look like, but I know who I want beside me while we discover it. Three years may sound short compared to couples who have been together for five, ten, or even more years. Some are already building their own families and creating a different chapter in their lives. But I will never compare our story to theirs. Because we have our own story. And our story is the one I love the most. For three years, the trust is still there. The loyalty is still there. The respect is still there. And most importantly, the unconditional love is still there. I know we are not a perfect couple. We argue. We misunderstand each other. We have difficult days. We face problems that sometimes feel bigger than us. But whenever challenges come, we always find our way back to the good memories, the laughter, the little victories, the battles we survived, and all the moments we conquered together. And somehow, through all of it, love and respect always bring us back to each other. That is what makes our relationship special to me. We don't have to be perfect. We just have to continue choosing each other. Again and again. Through the good days and the bad days. Through winning and losing. Through happiness and sadness. Through success and failure. Through every up and down that life gives us. And above everything else, we always put God at the center of our relationship. There were times when He gave us bigger battles than we expected. There were moments when we questioned why certain things were happening. But He never failed to help us. He continued to guide us, protect us, and give us strength. That is why we will continue to praise Him through success or defeat, through winning or losing, through happiness or sadness, and through every up and down. We will continue to have faith in Him, love Him, and respect Him, because everything we have today is a blessing from Him. And as we continue building our future together, I hope we never forget where we started, how far we've come, and how many things we have already conquered together. Three years down. And hopefully, a lifetime to go. I would still choose you. In every lifetime. In every situation. In every version of our story. I would still choose your hand to hold. So let's keep holding on. Let's keep growing. Let's keep choosing each other. Let's keep God at the center. And someday, when we look back at these three years, I hope we can smile and say,"We made it. We really made it." Happy 3rd Anniversary, my love. Thank you for being my partner, my safe place, my biggest support, and the person I choose to build my future with. I love you, always and unconditionally. ❤️

You.
